About the positionsWhere robots learn the real world

The Intelligent Bio-Robotic Systems (IBRS) group at the Munich Institute of Robotics and Machine Intelligence (MIRMI) of the Technical University of Munich, led by Prof. Lorenzo Masia, is looking for researchers with a PhD in Robotics, AI or a related field.

We are filling multiple postdoctoral positions, paid according to the German public-sector pay scale TV-L E13 (Stufe 3–4). You will work in the TUM RoboGym powered by NEURA, a center built jointly by TUM and NEURA Robotics for training humanoids and other robots at scale.

Research topicsWhat you could work on

Your research statement should address one of these topics.

Learning from human demonstration

Turning human motion and skill into robot behaviour.

Robot foundation models

Large, general models that transfer across tasks and embodiments.

Dexterous manipulation & humanoid control

Hands, whole-body control and contact-rich tasks.

Reinforcement learning & sim-to-real

Training in simulation and deploying on real hardware.

Safe Physical AI for human-robot interaction

Robots that work safely with and around people.

Your profileWe are looking for candidates who can

  • Lead an original research line within the TUM RoboGym
  • Coordinate and write research projects in robotics and AI

Requirements

  • PhD in Robotics, AI or a related field
  • A research vision on one of the listed topics
